NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently asked questions

Things to confirm before hiring a vehicle.

What documents do I need?

Requirements depend on the vehicle and hire terms. Usually, renters need a valid driving licence and accepted identification. Confirm the requirements with the provider.

Can I hire a vehicle with a driver?

Yes, request a chauffeur-driven option through the booking form. Availability and driver fees must be confirmed.

Are the prices final?

No. The rates on this demo page are examples. Confirm insurance, deposit, mileage, fuel, taxes and extra fees in writing.

How do I change a booking?

Contact the provider and check the cancellation and amendment policy in your agreement.
